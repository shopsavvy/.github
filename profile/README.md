## ShopSavvy

**[shopsavvy.com](https://shopsavvy.com)**: product lookup, current prices and price history for developers and AI agents. Comparing prices since 2008.

### What we do

- **[Data API](https://shopsavvy.com/data)**: send a barcode (UPC/EAN/ISBN), ASIN, URL, model number or MPN; get the product, current offers from tens of thousands of retailers, and price history back to 2011 (dense from 2019). [Docs](https://shopsavvy.com/data/documentation) · [OpenAPI](https://api.shopsavvy.com/v1/openapi.json)
- **[MCP server](https://shopsavvy.com/agents)**: gives Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other assistants product lookup, prices and price history. Free, no signup: `https://api.shopsavvy.com/mcp`
